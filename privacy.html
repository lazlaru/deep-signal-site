Privacy Policy

Deep Signal · Effective November 19, 2026

Deep Signal is a game created by LD Clinton. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Deep Signal on Mac, iPhone, iPad or Apple TV. It is the same on every device.

Information we collect

None. Deep Signal does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party services, and the game makes no network requests while you play.

Data saved on your device

The game saves your progress and settings on the device you play on (your Mac, iPhone, iPad or Apple TV) so you can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves that device and is not synced or backed up by the game. You can erase it at any time with Reset progress in the game's Options, or by deleting the app.

Game controllers

On Apple TV you play with the Siri Remote or a game controller, and you can use a game controller on other devices too. The game reads their buttons and sticks only to steer the sub while you play. Nothing about your remote, your controller or how you play is recorded or sent anywhere.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the Deep Signal support page.
