Deep Signal Support

Help for Deep Signal on iPhone, iPad and Mac

Get help

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub and include your device (for example iPhone 17 or MacBook Air) and its system version. Your saved game stays on that device, so please say what you were doing when it happened.

How to play on Mac

Steer and move
W A S D or the arrow keys steer. Hold Space to thrust and Q to reverse.
Lights
F turns your lights on and off. Some things hunt by light.
Sonar
E sends a sweep. Hold E and let go for a stun pulse.
Tools
R launches the robot, runs the sand blower on the reef and fires the heat lance in the ice.
Everything else
Esc pauses, N toggles music, M mutes everything, I inverts up and down.

How to play on iPhone and iPad

Steer and move
Steer with the stick on the left. Hold Thrust to move and Back to reverse.
Lights and sonar
Tap Lights to turn your lights on and off. Tap Ping to send a sweep, or hold it and let go for a stun pulse.
Tools
The tool button is named for what it does where you are: the robot, the sand blower on the reef or the heat lance in the ice.
Pause
Tap Pause to stop the game. Options is there too.

Questions

How do I stop playing and come back later?
On a Mac, go back to the ship, click Lunch break, then Quit game. Your progress is saved, and Continue brings you back. Command-Q also quits, but anything aboard the sub during a dive is lost. On iPhone and iPad, get back to the ship first, then leave the app.
I'm stuck in the ice.
Hold Q to back out along the glowing line you laid on the way in, or hold R to melt the spot you're wedged in. Pausing also gives you Call for a tow, which brings you home without what you were carrying.
How do I get to the last expedition?
Finish all four wrecks and it opens on the chart table.
Can I play in full screen on a Mac?
Yes. Choose View › Enter Full Screen, or press Control Command F.
Can I reset my progress?
Yes, with Reset progress in Options. Click it twice to confirm.
Does the game collect my data?
No. See the privacy policy.
